Upload to Google Drive

No client ID or app authorization needed. Download the selected photos as a ZIP, then upload it through Google Drive. To share individual photos, extract the ZIP and upload the extracted folder instead.

In Drive: New → File upload (ZIP) or Folder upload (extracted photos). Then right-click the uploaded item → Share → Copy link.

Exports use the app’s photo copies, up to 1600 pixels; they are not guaranteed originals. Uploads use your Google storage. Personal accounts include 15 GB shared across Drive, Gmail, and Photos; more storage is optional and paid.
